PUMKHAT Support

PUMKHAT helps churches stay connected: members, departments, events, chats, prayer and announcements.

Need help? Email us at pumkhat@biblehrampi.com. Tell us what happened and which phone you use, and we’ll reply as soon as we can.

Contact us

Email: pumkhat@biblehrampi.com. We are reachable by email only. If your question is about your account, please write from the email address you use to sign in.

Delete your account

You can delete your account inside the app:

  1. Tap Profile in the bottom bar.
  2. Tap Account & Security.
  3. Tap Review deletion. You’ll see what will be removed.
  4. Tap Delete account to confirm.

If you own a church, transfer ownership to someone else first. Your sign-in, private profile details, profile photo, church memberships and device registrations are removed. Things you added to shared church spaces stay, shown as from a “Former member”. Our Privacy Policy explains this in full.

If you can’t open the app, email pumkhat@biblehrampi.com from the email address on your account and we’ll delete it for you.

Common questions

I forgot my password.

On the sign-in screen, tap “Forgot password?” and enter the email you signed up with. We’ll email you a link to set a new password. If it doesn’t arrive within a few minutes, check your spam folder.

I didn’t get the email to confirm my account.

Check your spam or junk folder for an email from pumkhat@biblehrampi.com. If it still isn’t there, email us at pumkhat@biblehrampi.com from the address you signed up with.

How do I join my church?

Ask a leader at your church for its invite code and enter it in the app. You can also search for your church with Find a Church and ask to join; a leader approves your request.

Can I use PUMKHAT in my language?

Yes. PUMKHAT is available in English, Hakha Chin, Chinglish and Burmese. You can choose your language in the app.

How do I report a message or block someone?

Press and hold a message to see what you can do with it, including reporting it where reporting is available. You can also block people. Reports from the app go privately to your church’s moderators, who can remove messages and mute people. You can also email us about anything that worries you.

Is PUMKHAT free?

Yes. PUMKHAT is free to download and use, with no ads. If paid features are ever added, the price will be shown clearly before anyone pays.

Who can see what I post?

Mostly the people in your church, or the people in a chat or group you post to. Our Privacy Policy explains who can see what.
